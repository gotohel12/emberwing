# ARVEX Design System

The source of truth is `public/arvex.css`. The game and the launcher both load it, so a change there updates both.

## Identity
- **Mark:** a peak-shaped **A** with a camp notch (a small triangle) above a split horizon bar and an ember. It reads as a mountain, a shelter and a fire, which is the survival loop.
- **Wordmark:** custom-drawn letters, not a font. The **A** and the **V** mirror each other and both carry the camp notch. **R** and **E** have the same chamfered corner as the mark.
- **Lockups:** horizontal (mark | wordmark), stacked (mark over wordmark over SURVIVAL), mark only. Each comes in dark, light and mono versions.
- **Asset chain:** Logo → LINE → Main Banner (1920×640 and Discord 1920×1080) → Server PFP (1024², chamfered badge) → Support Banner, plus app icons and a Windows .ico. All are generated by `tools/brand.py` from the same geometry and palette.

## Typography
- **Primary (display, branding):** Barlow Condensed 600/700, uppercase, +0.06em tracking. Arabic headings fall back to Noto Kufi Arabic.
- **Secondary (UI, body):** IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/600/700. One family covers both Arabic and Latin.
- **Compact (version, status, speed, size, ETA, metadata):** JetBrains Mono 500 with tabular numbers.
- **Scale:** hero 64 · h1 40 · h2 28 · h3 20 · body 15 · small 13 · xs 11.

## Shape, spacing, motion
- **Chamfer:** top-left and bottom-right corners are cut (10 px on panels, 6 px on controls). This is the mark's geometry. Rounded corners appear only on tiny chips and keycaps.
- **Spacing:** 4 px grid (4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64).
- **Borders and shadows:** 1 px inset ridge borders, plus two shadow elevations.
- **Motion:** 120 ms for feedback, 220 ms for transitions, 420 ms for screens, all on `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`. `prefers-reduced-motion` and the in-game "Reduce motion" setting are respected.

## Components and states
Every button has default, hover, pressed (`:active`), focus-visible, disabled, loading (`.is-loading`), success (`.is-success`) and error (`.is-error`) states. Variants are primary, ghost and danger, in sizes sm, lg and xl.

The system also defines: inputs, select, toggle, range, panel, card (`is-interactive`, `is-selected`), chips (ok/warn/err/info/signal, dot, pulse), keycap, progress (normal/paused/ok/err/indeterminate, striped while active), spinner, tooltip, toasts, modal, tabs, the LINE separator and the rule.

## Iconography
A custom 48-grid item illustration set and a monoline 24-grid UI glyph set (2 px stroke, square caps) live in `src/ui/icons.js`. No third-party icon font is used.
