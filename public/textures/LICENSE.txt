ARVEX SURVIVAL — surface textures

All photographed textures in this folder come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are released under CC0 1.0
(public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  grass      aerial_grass_rock        dirt       brown_mud_leaves_01     sand    aerial_beach_01
  rock       rock_face                bark       bark_brown_02           wood    weathered_brown_planks
  metal      rusty_metal_02           fabric     denim_fabric            leather leather_white
  stonewall  castle_brick_02_red

Each layer is stored as <name>_d.jpg (albedo with ambient occlusion multiplied in) and <name>_n.jpg
(OpenGL normal X/Y in red/green, roughness in blue), at 1024 px (1k/) and 2048 px (2k/). The large surfaces (grass, dirt, sand, rock, bark, wood, metal, stonewall)
also ship at 4096 px (4k/), packed with lighter JPEG compression so the normal maps stay clean.

skin_d / skin_n are generated by this project (tools/skin_texture.py).
foliage.png (leaf clusters, conifer branch, grass tuft, with alpha) is generated by this project (tools/foliage_atlas.py).

Firearm models (public/models/arvex_models.bin: item:ak, item:m4, item:rifle, item:smg, item:smg2, item:bolt, item:l96, item:shotgun,
item:dbarrel, item:revolver, item:pistol, item:python, item:cknife, att:silencer, att:scope, att:flashlight) and their pictures in
public/icons/items/: "Ultimate Gun Pack" by Quaternius, CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack —
scaled and fitted to the game's hands with tools/blender/arvex_cc0_guns.py.
